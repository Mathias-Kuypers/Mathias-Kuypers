Mathias Kuypers Belgum Hasselt

My artistic practice begins with the act of making. I create objects, sculptures, models, drawings and performances, which I document through photography, video and stop-motion. By manipulating, combining and transforming materials, I explore how new forms and worlds can emerge.

I work with everyday, often ephemeral materials that acquire new meanings when removed from their original context. Their unpredictability, their mutual relationships and their capacity for change form an important starting point. In contrast to this physical matter stands the digital image. Through photography, video, projection and stop-motion, objects and actions undergo a new transformation. What is tangible and temporary takes on a different existence in the digital realm.

In my work, I explore the tension between the organic and the constructed, the raw and the artificial, the imperfect and the controlled. I am drawn to what deviates from the norm, to forms that resist definition and materials that retain their own agency.

For me, making is a form of liberation. Through a personal search, I construct worlds in which fixed boundaries dissolve and new possibilities emerge. Organic worlds, where matter, image and movement are in a constant state of transformation. A place where imperfection is not a flaw, but a force.
