Kunst opstelten Exhibition (2023)

Kunst op stelten: Was a collaboration with RECORE, the former furniture workshop in Hasselt. The exhibition was an initiative by The School, an organization that provides space for artists in Hasselt. The spaces were elevated, hence the title “Kunst op stelten.” The exhibition was curated by Fee Veraghtert and Mathias Kuypers. (Recore Hasselt)
