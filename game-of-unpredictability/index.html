Game of unpredictability (2019)

Game of unpredictability is an interactive installation about my fascination for the dreamworld and reality. These two worlds, merged during my creative process into a world which consists of a total experience of chaos and structure, which symbolizes a reaction to the everyday repetitive structure. (kunstschip Hasselt) video projections, stop-motion videos, sculpture material: PUR, latex towels
